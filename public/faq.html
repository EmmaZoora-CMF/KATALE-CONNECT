FAQ

Common questions about cost, approvals, edits, and trust.

Is Katale Connect free?

Yes. The coursework scope proposes a free directory for local and informal vendors.

Do vendors need accounts?

No. Vendors submit a form and wait for review. There is no login or dashboard in this prototype.

What documents are needed?

Vendors should upload a Trading License and Certificate of Incorporation so the team can confirm that the listing is genuine.

How are listings approved?

Submissions are checked for clear business details, location, contact information, verification documents, and acceptable use.

How can information be corrected?

Use Contact / Support to request an edit or report a listing that looks false or outdated.
