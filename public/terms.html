Terms of Use

Rules for responsible use, listing accuracy, and moderation.

Acceptable Listings

Listings should describe real vendors, services, locations, hours, contact details, and valid verification documents. False, duplicate, abusive, unsafe, or unverifiable listings may be removed.

Customer Responsibility

Customers should verify details directly with vendors before visiting, buying, or arranging services.

Moderation

Katale Connect may review submissions, Trading Licenses, Certificates of Incorporation, and user reports to keep the directory useful and trustworthy.

Prototype Scope

This coursework site demonstrates information access. It does not process payments, deliveries, or account management.
