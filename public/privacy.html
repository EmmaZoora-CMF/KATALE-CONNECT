Privacy Policy

This page explains how basic listing and support information is handled in the prototype.

Information Collected

Katale Connect collects business name, category, location, phone contact, WhatsApp contact, hours, short description, Trading License, Certificate of Incorporation, and support messages submitted through forms.

How Information Is Used

Listing data is used to display vendor profiles and help customers contact vendors directly. Verification documents are used only during approval to confirm that a vendor or business is genuine. Support messages are used to respond to questions, corrections, and reports.

Data Limits

The coursework prototype does not include payments, delivery tracking, vendor accounts, or dashboards.
