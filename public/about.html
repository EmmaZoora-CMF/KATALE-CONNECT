About / How It Works

Katale Connect helps informal vendors in Kampala become visible online through a free, mobile-first web directory.

The Problem

Many market sellers, roadside shops, tailors, repair technicians, and food vendors depend on walk-in traffic and word of mouth. Customers lose time asking around, while vendors miss buyers who search online first.

The Solution

Vendors submit basic listing information. Customers browse by category, location, or keyword, then contact vendors directly by phone or WhatsApp.

Process

Find

Search, browse categories, or choose a Kampala location.

Compare

Open vendor profiles to check category, area, hours, description, approval status, and document review status.

Contact

Call, WhatsApp, or use directions to reach the vendor directly.
